<p>In this episode of <em>On Rails</em>, <a href='https://www.linkedin.com/in/nodunayo/'>Nadia Odunayo</a>, founder and solo developer of <a href='https://thestorygraph.com'>The StoryGraph</a>, joins us to share what it really takes to build and maintain a Rails application used by over four million readers across web and mobile.</p><p>We discuss lessons from launching a PWA, shifting to <a href='https://turbo.hotwired.dev/handbook/native'>Turbo Native</a> for cross-platform support, and navigating challenges like in-app purchases and data syncing between systems. Nadia also talks about the bold decision to move from Heroku to <a href='https://www.yugabyte.com/'>YugabyteDB</a> to support growing workloads, and why she decided against hiring a team to manage it.</p><p>We also explore what it means to remain a solo developer by choice, the value of the Rails community, and why <a href='https://rubyonrails.org'>Ruby on Rails</a> continues to be the framework that powers her entire business. </p><p><b>Platforms &amp; Distribution</b></p><ul><li><a href='https://thestorygraph.com/'>The StoryGraph</a> – Nadia’s solo-built reading and book tracking platform </li><li><a href='https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-storygraph/id1565176753'>Apple App Store – The StoryGraph</a></li><li><a href='https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thestorygraph.thestorygraph'>Google Play Store – StoryGraph</a> </li><li><a href='https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps'>Progressive Web Apps (MDN)</a> – Alternative mobile delivery method initially explored</li></ul><p><b>Tools, Libraries &amp; Services</b></p><ul><li><a href='https://rubyonrails.org'>Ruby on Rails</a> – The primary framework powering<a href='https://thestorygraph.com'> The StoryGraph</a> </li><li><a href='https://turbo.hotwired.dev/handbook/native'>Turbo / Hotwire Native</a> – Used to support mobile apps with shared Rails backend </li><li><a href='https://github.com/heartcombo/devise'>Devise (GitHub)</a> – Authentication library for Rails </li><li><a href='https://sidekiq.org'>Sidekiq</a> – Background job processor </li><li><a href='https://github.com/instacart/makara'>Makara (GitHub)</a> – Database proxy for master/replica load splitting </li><li><a href='https://www.heroku.com'>Heroku</a> – Former hosting platform for The StoryGraph </li><li><a href='https://www.yugabyte.com'>YugabyteDB</a> – Postgres-compatible distributed database chosen for scaling </li><li><a href='https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/queries-with.html'>CTEs (Common Table Expressions)</a> – Used for optimizing queries across distributed data</li><li><a href='https://linear.app/'>Linear</a> – Project management tool currently used by Nadia</li></ul><p><b>Books</b></p><ul><li><a href='https://pragprog.com/titles/psruby/ruby-under-a-microscope/'><em>Ruby Under a Microscope</em> by Pat Shaughnessy</a> – A deep dive into Ruby internals </li></ul>
