<p>In this episode of On Rails, Robby is joined by Aaron Patterson, better known online as <a href='https://github.com/tenderlove'>Tenderlove</a>, a senior staff engineer on<a href='https://www.shopify.com/'> Shopify</a>’s Ruby infrastructure team and a longtime contributor to both Ruby and Rails Core. Aaron and Robby chat about how <a href='https://docs.ruby-lang.org/en/master/Ractor.html'>Ractors</a><b> </b>are finally bringing true multi-core parallelism to Ruby, the proposal Aaron has pitched to<a href='https://rubygems.org/'> RubyGems.org</a> for content addressable gems that could make bundle install dramatically faster, and the real reasons gems reach for native C extensions in the first place.</p><p>They also have a candid conversation about how Aaron actually uses AI day to day, from digging through unfamiliar code at Shopify&apos;s scale to building a JPEG encoder almost entirely with Claude, and why he still can&apos;t quite bring himself to trust it the way he trusts a longtime teammate.</p><p><b>Links</b></p><p><a href=' https://github.com/tenderlove'>Tenderlove</a><br/><a href='https://www.shopify.com/'>Shopify</a> <br/><a href='https://docs.ruby-lang.org/en/master/Ractor.html'>Ractors</a> <br/><a href='https://rubygems.org/'>RubyGems.org</a> <br/><a href='https://github.com/ffi/ffi'>FFI</a> </p>
