<p>In this episode of On Rails, Robby is joined by<a href='https://www.linkedin.com/in/jaytennier/'> Jay Tennier</a>, Engineering Manager at <a href='https://www.rainforestqa.com/'>Rainforest QA</a>, where he&apos;s spent over seven years working across a long-lived Rails monolith and supporting services. They explore how Rainforest maintains their platform with a small team, and the practical decisions that come with that reality. Jay shares lessons from pulling microservices back into the monolith, why they wrap third-party services in adapters, and how they push analytics work to BigQuery instead of straining their Rails database. The conversation covers testing philosophy including &quot;wet tests&quot; over DRY abstractions, using dry-monads for complex service flows, and how celebrating code deletion has become part of their engineering culture.</p><p><b>Social + Web Presence</b></p><p>LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/jaytennier/<br/>GitHub: https://github.com/jaytennie/ </p><p>Twitter/X: https://x.com/jaytennier<br/>Bluesky: https://bsky.app/profile/jaytennier.bsky.social</p><p><b>Company/Org Links</b></p><p>Homepage: https://www.rainforestqa.com/</p><p><b>Tools &amp;
