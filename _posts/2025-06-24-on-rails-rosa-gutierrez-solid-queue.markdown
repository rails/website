<p>In this episode of ‘On Rails’, host Robby Russell (@planetargon) chats with Rosa Gutiérrez, Principal Programmer at <a href='https://37signals.com/'>37signals</a>, about the technical decisions behind Solid Queue - a database-backed job queue replacing Resque in their Rails apps.</p><p>Rosa dives into why her team built <a href='https://github.com/rails/solid_queue'>Solid Queue</a>, how it improves reliability, visibility, and maintainability, and the challenges of migrating live apps like <a href='https://www.hey.com/'>Hey</a> during active development. Learn how they tackled recurring jobs, long-running tasks, and testing strategies, plus insights on system design, scaling, and the joy of deleting old code.</p><p>Topics:</p><ul><li>Why 37signals replaced <a href='https://github.com/resque/resque'>Resque</a></li><li>Building a job queue with <a href='https://guides.rubyonrails.org/active_job_basics.html'>ActiveJob</a> + MySQL</li><li>Transparent job states &amp; using <a href='https://github.com/rails/mission_control-jobs'>Mission Control</a> as a dashboard</li><li>Migrating with minimal impact</li><li>Best practices for recurring and long-running jobs</li><li>Recommended tools, testing gems, and dev books</li></ul><p>Links:</p><ul><li>Rosa&apos;s <a href='https://gist.github.com/rosa/57041c184068a76a77141c8b2765c511'>migration script for scheduled jobs</a> from Resque to Solid Queue</li><li><a href='https://github.com/fractaledmind/acidic_job'>Acidic Job</a></li><li><a href='https://github.com/fractaledmind/chaotic_job'>Chaotic Job</a></li><li><a href='https://rosa.codes/'>Rosa.codes</a></li><li><a href='https://www.rubyevents.org/speakers/rosa-gutierrez'>Rosa’s videos on Ruby Events</a></li><li><a href='https://dev.37signals.com/author/rosa/'>Rosa on the 37signals Dev blog</a></li><li><a href='https://github.com/rosa'>Rosa on Github</a></li><li><a href='https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosagutierrezescudero/'>Rosa on LinkedIn</a></li><li>Book Recommendation: <a href='https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html'><em>Refactoring</em>, Second Edition</a> by Martin Fowler<br/><br/></li></ul><p>#RubyOnRails #SolidQueue #BackgroundJobs #37signals #OnRailsPodcast</p>
