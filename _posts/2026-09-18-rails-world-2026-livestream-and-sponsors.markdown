As we gear up for [Rails World 2026](/world/2026) next week, we're so grateful to the companies showing up to make the first Rails World in the US a great one. 

Below you’ll find every company sponsoring Rails World, and what they are covering (because sponsorship involves so much more than just booths!) 

## But first, the best news

This year thanks to our Event Partner **Shopify**, we will livestream the **Rails World Opening Keynote** for the very first time. 

DHH will lay out his most defining vision yet for what comes next for Rails, and what it means for all of us who build software with it. At this pivotal moment in technology, this is one not to miss.

Tune in early for a pre-opening chat hosted by Shopify's **Andrew Novoselac** and **Adrianna Chang**, and a foreword by **Amanda Perino**, Executive Director of the [Rails Foundation](/foundation).

**When**: Wed, Sept 23

**Times**:
- 7:30 AM Pacific
- 9:30 AM Austin
- 10:30 AM Eastern
- 3:30 PM UK
- 4:30 PM Central Europe
- 8:15 PM Mumbai
- 11:30 PM Japan

[Set your reminder and tune in here on Wed Sept 23.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SxpJpHuus)

And now, back to our sponsors…
 
## Event Partner

**[Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/)** - We owe an enormous debt of gratitude to Shopify, our **Event Partner** for Rails World 2026. Their support is making all of the following possible:

* Hosting the very first **Rails World livestream** 
* Helping Matz get to Rails World for the Day 2 fireside chat
* Co-sponsoring the **Closing Party** with Procore
* Co-sponsoring the **Rails at Scale Summit** with Procore and Cloudflare
* Hosting the **Shopify Lounge** in the Sponsor area
* Covering **post-production** so that every session is edited and on YouTube within two weeks after the event
* They also sponsored the **props** this year. Because yes, this year the venue called for props. Those coming will have to see what that means onsite next week.

* **[AppSignal](https://www.appsignal.com/)** - Attendees can stop by the AppSignal booth for stroopwafels, to play _sjoelen_, to catch a demo of AppSignal in action, and to enter a competition. The AppSignal booth is always a good time.
* **[Procore](https://www.procore.com/)** - Procore is sponsoring at the Platinum level. They also host the Rails at Scale Summit and are co-sponsoring the Closing Party with Shopify. They opted not to have a booth this year so their team could attend the sessions!

## Lounge Sponsor
 
* **[Dell](https://www.dell.com/) & [Intel](https://www.intel.com/)** - We’re excited to have **Dell & Intel** at Rails World this year. They will have a lounge with laptops, workstations, and monitors for attendees to check out (including seeing AI run on-device with Omarchy on XPS).
