<p><a href='https://www.linkedin.com/in/jason-meller-04498230/'>Jason Meller</a>, founder of Kolide (acquired by 1Password in 2023) and now VP of Product at <a href='https://1password.com/'>1Password</a>, joins Robby for a conversation about a career at the intersection of Rails, cybersecurity, and building.</p><p>They dig into why Rails has become one of the most token-efficient architectures for LLM-assisted development, and why that advantage matters as token costs increasingly shape what&apos;s worth building. </p><p>Jason also shares what he&apos;s learned about keeping developer environments secure as agentic tools become part of everyday workflows, covering 1Password&apos;s open-source SCAM benchmark, how LLMs handle credentials when operating autonomously, and practical steps developers, founders, and engineering leaders can take to stay ahead of it.</p><p><b>Tools &amp; Products</b></p><ul><li>1Password (https://1password.com)</li><li>Kolide (https://kolide.com)</li><li>Cursor (https://cursor.com)</li><li>Claude / Claude Opus by Anthropic (https://anthropic.com/claude)</li><li>OpenAI Codex (https://openai.com)</li><li>Lovable (https://lovable.dev)</li><li>CrowdStrike (https://crowdstrike.com)</li><li>GitLab (https://gitlab.com)</li><li>Oh My Zsh (https://ohmyzsh.sh)</li><li>Wiz (https://wiz.io)</li></ul><p><b>Projects &amp; Benchmarks</b></p><ul><li>SCAM Benchmark by 1Password (https://github.com/1Password/scam)</li><li>OpenClaw (open-source agentic AI tool)</li><li>Honest Security Manifesto (https://honest.security)</li><li>1Password Environments for Developers (https://developer.1password.com/docs/cli/secrets-environment-variables)</li><li>The Rails Foundation (https://rubyonrails.org/foundation)</li></ul><p><b>Books</b></p><ul><li>You Can Stop Stupid: Stopping Losses from Accidental and Malicious Actions by Ira Winkler &amp; Tracy Celaya Brown (https://www.amazon.com/dp/1119566711)</li></ul>
