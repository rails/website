<p>In this episode of On Rails, Robby is joined by <a href='https://www.linkedin.com/in/kaylareopelle/'>Kayla Reopelle,</a> a lead software engineer at <a href='https://newrelic.com/'>New Relic</a>, where she works on both the Ruby Agent and OpenTelemetry RubyGems. They explore what observability means for Rails developers—not just as a debugging tool, but as a way to build clearer, more reliable systems. Kayla explains OpenTelemetry&apos;s vendor-agnostic approach to instrumentation and shares practical ways to experiment with traces, metrics, and logs in both production and local
