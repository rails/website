<p>Robby is joined by<a href='https://shop-ware.com/'> Nikky Southerland</a>, Lead Software Engineer at<a href='https://shop-ware.com/'> Shop-Ware</a> — a cloud-based platform that helps independent automotive service shops run their businesses. Ruby on Rails has powered Shop-Ware since the first line of code was written back in 2013, and more than a decade later the codebase is still 90%+ Rails.</p><p>Robby talks with Nikky about what it actually takes to keep a mature Rails app moving with a team roughly half the size it used to be, including a frontend migration that&apos;s still in progress, the API decision they regretted almost immediately, and the LaunchDarkly kill switches that save them when a third-party integrator goes down.</p><p><br/></p>
