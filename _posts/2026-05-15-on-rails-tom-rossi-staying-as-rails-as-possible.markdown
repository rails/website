<p>Tom Rossi, co-founder of<a href='https://www.higherpixels.com/'> Higher Pixels</a> and the team behind<a href='https://www.buzzsprout.com/'> Buzzsprout</a>, joins Robby to talk about what it really looks like to stay &quot;as Rails as possible&quot;, purely out of pragmatism. With over 472,000 podcasts on the platform and a team of fewer than ten people, Tom explains how sticking to vanilla Rails has been the foundation of Buzzsprout&apos;s ability to move fast, stay lean, and keep up with a rapidly evolving industry.</p><p>In this episode, Tom walks through Buzzsprout&apos;s migration from Paperclip to Active Storage (including what broke spectacularly in production), their recent shift from hand-rolled summary tables to ClickHouse for analytics, and how Hotwire made building the Buzzsprout mobile app surprisingly manageable. He also shares how Buzzsprout&apos;s real-world scale, including the monkey patching that resulted from it, led directly to contributions back upstream into Rails itself.</p><ul><li>Higher Pixels: https://www.higherpixels.com</li><li>Buzzsprout: https://www.buzzsprout.com</li><li>Buzzsprout Blog: https://www.buzzsprout.com/blog</li><li>Tick (time tracking): https://www.tickspot.com</li><li>Donor Tools: https://www.donortools.com</li><li>StreamCare: https://www.streamcare.com</li><li>Higher Pixels joins the Rails Foundation: https://rubyonrails.org/2025/2/18/higher-pixels-joins-foundation</li></ul>
